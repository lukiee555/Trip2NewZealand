Vehicles & road access

Three cars first, then three Christchurch round-trip campervans.

🚙

Three medium SUVs — North Island

Collect at Auckland Airport after the 17:50 arrival on 28 March and return at Auckland Airport on 1 April evening. Use three primary renters and attach the inbound flight number to every reservation.

🚐

Three campervans — South Island

Collect near Christchurch Airport on 2 April after the domestic flight and return on 17 April. Two adults per vehicle; process the three handovers in parallel.

Road-access controls

Rob Roy uses a commercial shuttle. Moke Lake, Crown Range and any gravel access require written permission in the signed rental agreement. Availability of a campsite does not itself grant road permission.

Visitor visa — salaried group of six

Prepare one clear evidence pack for every adult. Partners may be included together where eligible, but each applicant still needs personal identity, employment and financial evidence.

Target: Dec 2026–Jan 2027
01Apply online

Use Immigration Online with a RealMe account. Apply individually or include an eligible partner in the same application.

02Passport validity

Official minimum: valid for at least 3 months after leaving New Zealand. Six months is a prudent personal buffer, not an INZ rule.

03Official funds test

At least NZD 1,000 per person per month, or NZD 400 when accommodation is already paid, plus onward-travel funds.

04Current guide

Cost starts from NZD 441 and 80% are processed within 2 weeks. Recheck the portal and allow extra time during the November–March peak.

Salaried-applicant document pack

Open the detailed checklist and official Immigration New Zealand links.

Prepare for each applicant

  • Passport scan and one compliant digital visa photograph taken within the last 6 months.
  • A concise cover letter explaining the holiday purpose, dates, companions, funding, reasons to return to India and any previous visa refusals.
  • Employer letter or NOC confirming role, approved leave and the expected return-to-work date.
  • Recent salary slips and salary-credit evidence; 6 months of personal bank statements; Form 16 and ITRs for the last 2 years where available.
  • Final itinerary, flight reservation or planned flight details, and available hotel, campervan, campground and activity bookings.
  • Return ticket or evidence of funds to buy one; original documents plus acceptable English translations for anything not issued in English.

Submission quality checks

  • Keep travel dates, employment details, funding and the payer explanation consistent across all six applications.
  • Explain the source of any recent large credits; avoid unexplained deposits immediately before applying.
  • Upload clear, readable PDFs, combine related pages logically and avoid duplicate or irrelevant files.
  • Do not rely on informal ₹4–6 lakh or “double the trip cost” formulas; use the official NZD threshold and a credible overall financial history.
  • Avoid non-refundable bookings until approval, and disclose previous refusals or immigration issues honestly.
Suggested group setup: three couple applications where partnership eligibility is clear, or six individual applications. Use one shared itinerary and booking pack, but retain personal employment and financial evidence for every adult.
