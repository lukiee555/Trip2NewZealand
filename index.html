Campervan booking

One six-berth vehicle is possible but tight for six adults. Compare the real bed layout, storage and payload - not only the marketing capacity.

Auckland 29 Mar - Christchurch 17 Apr

Vehicle non-negotiables

  • 6 legal travel seats and 6 berths
  • Current green self-containment warrant
  • Automatic transmission, heating, toilet and shower
  • Exact height / length for ferry and campsites
  • Enough payload and storage for six soft bags

Contract checks

  • One-way fee and branch hours
  • Zero / low excess cover, windscreen and tyres
  • All drivers added and licences accepted
  • Roadside recovery and towing exclusions
  • Cancellation / change terms around visa timing
  • Airline / NZ import check before carrying toilet chemicals

Written road approvals

  • Rob Roy / Raspberry Creek gravel and fords
  • Moke Lake gravel access
  • Lilybank Road
  • Crown Range Road - many rentals prohibit it
  • Any unsealed road over the contract threshold

Tracks that normally do not need an entry ticket

Rob Roy, Isthmus Peak, Hooker Valley, Tasman viewpoints and Blue Pools are condition-led rather than ticket-led. The full Hooker Valley Track is currently open to Hooker Lake after the new upper suspension bridge opened in July 2026; still recheck DOC alerts 24 hours before and again on the morning. White Horse Hill campground is different - it does require a campsite booking.

Visitor visa process

Planning assumption: Indian passport holders require a New Zealand Visitor Visa. Verify every traveller's exact passport and circumstances on Immigration New Zealand.

Official visa page

1. Prepare - Sep to Nov 2026

  • Passports valid at least 3 months after planned departure
  • Employment / business evidence and approved leave
  • Bank statements and proof of trip funds
  • Relationship documents if partners apply together
  • Draft itinerary and accommodation plan

2. Apply - target Jan 2027

  • Create / use the official online account
  • Upload clear colour scans and compliant photos
  • Show onward travel plan and reasons to return
  • Add certified translations where required
  • Pay the displayed visa fee and applicable IVL

3. After submission

  • Monitor messages and respond quickly
  • Do not assume approval because flights are held
  • Buy comprehensive travel insurance
  • After approval, lock non-refundable bookings
  • Carry visa approval and supporting documents offline
Official planning figures - recheck at payment

Immigration New Zealand currently lists Visitor Visa cost from NZD 441 and says 80% are processed within about two weeks; many visitors also pay a NZD 100 International Visitor Levy. The application must show enough funds and a credible plan to leave. Fees and processing times can change before 2027.
