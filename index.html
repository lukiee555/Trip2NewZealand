Timing policy used throughout

Ordinary days use wake and departure windows, not military-style minute rules. Early alarms appear only for a booked attraction, flight, ferry, dawn objective, tide window, long alpine access or the international departure.

Private-camp late arrival rule

Reaching a private campground at 19:00, 20:00 or later can be fine only when late self check-in or a late-arrival procedure is confirmed in advance. DOC sites and individual properties can have different rules.

Official visa page

Prepare

  • Passports valid for the required period
  • Employment or business evidence and leave approval
  • Bank statements and proof of trip funds
  • Relationship documents where relevant
  • Draft route and accommodation plan

Apply

  • Use the official online account
  • Upload clear colour scans and compliant photographs
  • Provide onward travel and reasons to return
  • Add certified translations when required
  • Pay the displayed fee and applicable levy

After submission

  • Monitor requests and respond quickly
  • Do not treat a held flight as visa approval
  • Buy comprehensive travel insurance
  • Carry approval and evidence offline
  • Recheck entry rules before travel
